Commit0110 - Detection and Session Stability

1. Auto Correct detection
   - Added a conservative Blob detector for broad off-centre high-energy regions.
   - Excludes the DC centre and isolated pixels.
   - Applies area and compactness guards to avoid normal encoding energy.
   - Blob candidates use the existing validated virtual-compensation and quality-ranking path.

2. Unified current-session visibility
   - Added a Session status panel.
   - Shows current mask region count and pixel count.
   - Shows current Artifact Removal, Image Detail, and Protection values.
   - Shows whether the reconstructed preview is current or needs recalculation.

3. Workflow continuity
   - Auto Correct result remains the restore point.
   - Quick Adjust recalculates once from original RAW + current mask + current values.
   - Paint and Expert use Start Manual Paint-equivalent state and retain the current Auto mask.
   - Editing the mask marks the preview as needing recalculation without losing the mask.
