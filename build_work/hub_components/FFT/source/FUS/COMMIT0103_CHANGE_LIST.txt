MR Image Explorer RC1 Commit0103 Change List

1. Auto Correct orchestration
- Added six automatic retry trials around the existing Auto Correct engine.
- Did not refactor candidate detection, validation, compensation, or quality calculation.

2. Trial presets
- Trial 1: Removal 50 / Detail 75 / Protection 85
- Trial 2: Removal 60 / Detail 75 / Protection 75
- Trial 3: Removal 70 / Detail 75 / Protection 65
- Trial 4: Removal 80 / Detail 75 / Protection 55
- Trial 5: Removal 90 / Detail 75 / Protection 45
- Trial 6: Removal 100 / Detail 75 / Protection 35

3. Retry and selection
- Retry when no validated candidate exists or Quality is below 60.
- Best Quality selected after all completed trials.
- Tie-break order: lower Image Change, lower Residual Artifact, higher Detail Preservation, higher Artifact Reduction.

4. UI
- Progress shows Searching Artifacts, Trial n / 6, and Evaluating Quality.
- Reliable result completes without requiring Quick Adjust.
- Unreliable final result offers Manual Paint, Expert Settings, and Close.

5. Metadata
- version.json updated to Commit0103 / version 5.32.0.
- BUILD_INFO.txt and README.md updated.
